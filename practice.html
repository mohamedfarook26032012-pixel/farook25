# **The System of Islam ☪️** Islam is a complete way of life that teaches people to live with faith, kindness, justice, honesty, and discipline. The Islamic system is based on the teachings of the Holy Quran and the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). It guides Muslims in every aspect of life, including worship, family, education, business, social relationships, and personal responsibilities. Islam encourages helping the poor, respecting parents, treating others fairly, speaking the truth, and maintaining peace in society. Its five pillars—Shahada, Salah, Zakat, Sawm, and Hajj—form the foundation of Muslim life. The ultimate purpose of Islam is to worship Allah, follow His guidance, develop good character, and work towards success in this life and the Hereafter.
